# Sidebar artwork

`team-headshots.png` is original artwork generated for DeepSeek Office with the built-in
image generation tool on 2026-10-20. It is a transparent 1254 × 1254 PNG with sixteen
portraits in a 4 × 4 atlas. No third-party images were used as inputs. The selected
output is retained unchanged, including its alpha channel. Project license: Apache-2.0.

The office building / conversation mark is original vector artwork in
`src/client/sidebar-art.js`, authored for this project.

The client assigns a stable atlas cell from the Bot identity and existing character
shape. Existing uploaded images take precedence. Existing Bot status motion and badges
are reused. The atlas is installed once as a CSS background; no image model runs while
the app is in use.

## Generation prompt

Create ONE production-ready square avatar sprite atlas for an original cozy anime
office game, a coherent illustrated asset sheet containing exactly 16 different
original chibi anime head-and-shoulder portraits arranged in a strict 4 by 4 grid of
equal square cells. Each portrait perfectly centered within its own cell, same scale
and eye line, whole hairstyle visible, generous transparent gutter around each head;
no portrait crosses its cell. Transparent background throughout, no grid lines, no
text, no logos, no badges, no symbols or extra decorations. Style: simple but
beautifully hand drawn anime big-head profile icons, friendly expressive eyes, bold
tidy dark outlines, two-tone cel shading, small shoulders, playful professional adults,
instantly readable at 32 pixels. Do not use geometric placeholder faces. Diverse
genders and skin tones, deliberately different face silhouettes, hair shapes, clothing
and colors across all sixteen. Row 1: black short tousled hair warm beige skin blue
shirt; brown bob fair skin terracotta cardigan; white short swept hair dark brown skin
teal jacket; auburn high bun tan skin cream top. Row 2: navy long hair fair skin sage
shirt; golden curly hair medium brown skin plum sweater; black cropped curls deep brown
skin mustard shirt; lavender pixie light skin dark blue top. Row 3: red wavy shoulder
hair freckled light skin sea green shirt; dark brown short side part tan skin cream
blazer with round glasses; mint bob warm beige skin peach top; dark plum braided hair
brown skin blue cardigan. Row 4: silver long hair pale skin burgundy top; black ponytail
medium tan skin turquoise shirt; sandy short messy hair fair skin olive jacket;
chestnut round curly bob deep brown skin lilac shirt. Keep all portraits charming and
distinct while maintaining identical line weight, lighting and framing. This is a
single tightly registered 4x4 game UI avatar atlas, no scene or presentation mockup.
