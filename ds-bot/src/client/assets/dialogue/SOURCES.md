# Dialogue portraits

`coworker-busts.png` is original art generated for DeepSeek Office with the
built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-20. It depicts three adult coworkers
(short tousled hair, bob, long hair) on a transparent, three-column atlas.
No reference artwork or third-party character was used.

Generation brief: polished original anime coworker half-body portraits for a
cozy modern-office visual novel; equal columns and consistent framing; purple
hair, teal jackets, warm peach skin and ivory shirts; thin linework and soft cel
shading; no text, logos, accessories, background or geometric stand-ins.

The client classifies the authored purple, cyan and peach regions and replaces
their colors with the saved scene wardrobe, retaining shading and alpha. The
source atlas is bundled locally. No image model or network request runs when a
portrait is displayed or recolored. Hairstyles map to three approximate
silhouettes; this is not a reconstruction of every possible 3D haircut.

scale/baseline. Left column: androgynous person with short tousled hair. Center

polished 2D anime illustration, precise thin dark linework, softly painted cel

Important palette for automatic regional recoloring: ALL hair saturated
violet-purple (roughly #975bc7 with purple shadows and highlights), ALL jackets

natural warm peach (roughly #efc4a2). Distinct clean boundaries for
hair/skin/jacket, no purple anywhere except hair, no teal anywhere except jackets.
Eyes neutral brown, ivory shirts. Preserve fine highlights and shading.
Transparent background with genuine alpha, no background panels, no scene, no
text, no grid, no labels, no accessories or logos, no drop shadow. Wide landscape
canvas, equal three columns, every portrait fully contained in its column and
separated from neighboring portraits. High quality collectible visual novel
