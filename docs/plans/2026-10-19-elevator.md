floor corridor, an adjacent destination panel with floor information, directional
travel animation, outgoing fade and incoming reveal. Extend the existing lift
interaction and floor navigation; preserve all agent/chat behavior.

Keep the current room grid. Reserve the rightmost 64×64 corridor footprint for
the cabin, with a west-facing entrance and a reachable center. Use the supplied
GLB, named DoorL/DoorR parts and procedural cutaway visibility so people remain
visible inside. Entering the center opens the panel automatically; clicking or
interacting with the lift walks there first. Closing the panel walks back out.

One controller owns closing → moving → loading → arriving → opening. Disable
duplicate trips and the current-floor button. Pause player input during panel
use/travel; do not pause real agent work. Outgoing scene fades and moves opposite
the trip direction, then navigation changes the existing office view. Hold the
cover until the destination renderer is ready; spawn inside its same cabin,
reveal, open doors and walk out. Reduced motion uses short opacity-only steps.

if they vanish or fail during loading. Bound loading time; cancel timers on close.

direct-navigation shortcut and is disabled during a trip.
